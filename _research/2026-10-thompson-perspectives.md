agInnovation South assessed 15 Southern universities and found that sustaining public plant breeding depends on institutions as much as individual breeders. Broad collaboration and crop diversity are strengths, but uneven capacity creates vulnerabilities. Priorities include planning for continuity, stewarding germplasm and knowledge, and sustaining partnerships.
